## Key guardrails from the sources
- GitHub may highlight genuinely beginner-friendly issues labeled good first issue, but labels alone do not create an active community.
- GitHub CODEOWNERS alone doesn't impose merge requirements. Configure branch rulesets/branch protection and check feature support for your account.
- HN Show HN is for projects people can actually try. HN explicitly discourages automatically generated submission copy and prohibits vote solicitation; founder should write the final HN post personally.
- Product Hunt permits sharing a launch but forbids directly asking people for upvotes and coordinated voting or incentives.
- Reddit prohibits repetitive unwanted posting. A 10% self-promotion practice exists in some communities; it is not an automatically universal rule.
- No campaign can guarantee Stars or product rankings.
- Consent for voices and license compatibility of third-party weights must be checked separately from social-media rules.

## Preflight check before an external post
- [ ] I read current rules, pinned megathreads and moderator guidance for THIS exact community.
- [ ] I have something original, useful and reproducible to share.
- [ ] All screenshots are actual UI, conceptual art is labeled conceptual.
- [ ] Benchmark figures have model/hardware/sample provenance.
- [ ] I avoid private voices, Kaggle secrets and API keys.
- [ ] I do not claim official affiliation with Qwen, GitHub, Blender, Applio, Product Hunt or any provider without evidence.
- [ ] I am not requesting or buying votes/stars, offering rewards for them, or bulk-DMing strangers.
- [ ] I can answer follow-up questions and direct interested people to a specific Issue.
